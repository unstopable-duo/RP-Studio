Our website uses Google Fonts to display text. By using our site, your browser may send your IP address and other diagnostic data to Google's servers in the United States. Our payments are processed securely by PayPal, and we do not store your financial information.

5. Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. If you wish to exercise these rights, please contact us.
